<p>From&nbsp;<strong>27 July 2026</strong>, parents/guardians of Singapore
Citizen students can access the MOE's&nbsp;<strong>Student Finance System (SFS)</strong>&nbsp;using&nbsp;<strong>Singpass</strong>&nbsp;to
view and manage their child’s/ward’s Edusave account.</p>
<p>To facilitate the use of Edusave funds for approved school programmes,
parents/guardians are required to complete a&nbsp;<strong>one-time setup of the Edusave Standing Order (SO)</strong>&nbsp;via
SFS. This one-time authorisation allows the school to deduct approved programme
fees from the student’s Edusave account once parental consent for participation
has been obtained.</p>
<p>After the Edusave SO has been set up, there is no need for parents/guardians
to repeat the process for future approved programmes. The SO will remain
in effect until it is terminated by the parent/guardian.</p>
<p>For more information, please refer to <a href="/edusave-standing-order/" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">Edusave Withdrawal Procedure for Singapore Citizens</a>
</p>
